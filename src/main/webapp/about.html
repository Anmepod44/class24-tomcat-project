About Class27

Distilling the essentials of modern software delivery — one clean pipeline at a time.

Class27 focuses on the full delivery lifecycle: build with Maven, store artifacts in Nexus, and deploy to Tomcat. Every concept is reinforced through repetition and real code — no fluff, no filler.

Our approach is simple: ship early, ship often, and learn from every deployment.

  • Code reviews and live demos every week
  • Deploy early, deploy often
  • Portfolio-ready projects by graduation
